# Playtest notes — 2026-10-15 (Milestone 2a build)

The user's notes from the first sessions on https://theaterofconquest.com, grouped by where they will likely land.

## Turn flow (Milestone 2b)
- **Turn start, production first.**
  - When a unit is completed at the start of your turn, the game says so and opens that city's production screen, so you can keep or change what it builds next.
  - It steps through each city that finished a unit.
- **Then units.** After production, the game steps through each unit that still has moves. The active unit **blinks** so it's obvious whose turn it is.
- **End of turn.**
  - When no units have moves left, the game says so and prompts you to end the turn.
  - The **End Turn** button blinks.

## Movement (Milestone 2b, together with Go-to and pathfinding)
- **Path preview.** After selecting a unit, clicking or hovering a destination shows the planned path and how many turns it will take: this turn, or the turn number it arrives on.

## Unit info panel (Milestone 2b)
- **Prominent active-unit panel.** The right-hand panel prominently shows the active (blinking) unit and whose turn it is.
- **Fields to show:**
  - unit type with a picture
  - a name
  - health
  - range/fuel
  - moves
  - cargo
- **Names.** Each unit gets a generated military-style name, such as "151st Infantry" instead of "Army #1". Optionally, players can give a unit their own name.
  - Generated names can be derived from the unit id and type, so they stay deterministic.
  - Custom names would need a new `renameUnit` command and a `name` field in state.
- **Clearer health.** "Hits 1/1" is confusing. Show damage in a clearer way, e.g. "Damage 0/1", or a health bar labelled with the unit's strength.

## Art (a later art pass)
- **City icon** that looks like a city (buildings or skyline in the owner's color), not a plain colored square.
- **Unit icons** that look like the unit: an infantry figure for Army, plane silhouettes, ship silhouettes, and so on, instead of letters.
