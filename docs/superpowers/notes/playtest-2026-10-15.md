# Playtest notes — 2026-10-15 (Milestone 2a build)

The user's notes from the first sessions on https://theaterofconquest.com, grouped by where they will likely land.

## Turn flow (Milestone 2b)
- **Turn start, production first.**
  - When a unit is completed at the start of your turn, the game says so and opens that city's production screen, so you can keep or change what it builds next.
  - It steps through each city that finished a unit.
- **Then units.** After production, the game steps through each unit that still has moves. The active unit **blinks** so it's obvious whose turn it is.
- **End of turn.**
  - When no units have moves left, the game says so and prompts you to end the turn.
  - The **End Turn** button blinks.

## Standing orders (Milestone 2b, high priority)
- **No Sentry yet.** Units must be moved, skipped (S only lasts one turn) or disbanded every turn. That gets tedious fast.
- **Sentry** keeps a unit idle across turns until something happens nearby (an enemy is sighted) or the player wakes it.
- **Ship 2b's other orders with it:** Go-to, Patrol, Explore.

## Movement (Milestone 2b, together with Go-to and pathfinding)
- **Path preview.** After selecting a unit, clicking or hovering a destination shows the planned path and how many turns it will take: this turn, or the turn number it arrives on.

## Message log (Milestone 2b)
- **Remove the list numbers** ("1.", "2.", "3."). They come from the log being an ordered list (`<ol>`), and they confuse people. Use a plain list.
- **Fewer turn lines.** Show the turn number only as a small header or divider, not a "Turn N: Player" line per turn. Showing every turn should be optional at most.

## Units inside cities (Milestone 2b, high priority)
- **New units are invisible on the map.** Units in a city aren't drawn; they're only listed in the city's side panel. Players don't notice new units.
  - Draw a unit badge on the city: the top unit's icon plus a count when several units are there.
  - A newly built unit should blink on top of its city, and be first in the turn's unit cycle.

## City names (Milestone 2b)
- **Real names, picked at random.** Each city gets a real-world city name at random, shown as a label on the map and in the city panel.
- **Editable.** A player can rename their own cities.
- **Implementation notes:**
  - Names are assigned by the map generator from the world seed, so a shared seed gives the same names.
  - They are stored as `City.name` in the game state; a captured city keeps its name.
  - Renaming is a new engine command, `renameCity`, allowed only for the current owner.
- **Name source:** city names are facts, so a list is free to use.
  - The simplest option is a curated list of about 500–1000 well-known names, kept in a data file in the repo.
  - If we pull from a dataset such as GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), credit it on an About/credits screen.
- **Region (decided):** the New Game screen gets an optional **name region** choice, e.g. World, Europe, North America, Asia. Leaving it unset picks names at random from the whole world list.
- **Also decide in 2b:**
  - Avoid duplicate names within one game.
  - Labels may need to hide at small zoom levels to avoid clutter.

## Map view (Milestone 2b)
- **Dynamic zoom.** Early on only a few squares are explored, so the screen is mostly black.
  - Auto-zoom to fit the explored area (plus a margin) so the playable part fills the view.
  - Zoom out step by step as more of the map is explored.
  - If the player zooms manually, keep their zoom until they reset it (e.g. a "fit" button or key).
  - This likely needs a larger maximum tile size than today's 48 px for the opening turns.

## Unit info panel (Milestone 2b)
- **Prominent active-unit panel.** The right-hand panel prominently shows the active (blinking) unit and whose turn it is.
- **Fields to show:**
  - unit type with a picture
  - a name
  - health
  - range/fuel
  - moves
  - cargo
- **Names.** Each unit gets a generated military-style name, such as "151st Infantry" instead of "Army #1". Optionally, players can give a unit their own name.
  - Generated names can be derived from the unit id and type, so they stay deterministic.
  - Custom names would need a new `renameUnit` command and a `name` field in state.
- **Clearer health.** "Hits 1/1" is confusing. Show damage in a clearer way, e.g. "Damage 0/1", or a health bar labelled with the unit's strength.

## Effects and sound (later: polish/art pass)
- **Battle animations:** at minimum, units slide between squares, there's a muzzle flash or explosion on combat, and a sinking or explosion effect when a unit is destroyed.
  - The main spec already calls for a brief combat animation.
  - 2a shows combat only in the message log.
- **Sound:** combat, unit built, city captured, turn start; plus a mute/volume control. Sound is on the main spec's "later" list.
- **Per-unit movement sounds:** each unit type has its own sound when it moves, as in the original game. Examples: marching boots, a tank engine, a jet whoosh, a ship's engine or horn.
  - Sounds must be our own: newly made, generated, or openly licensed. None may be taken from the original game.

## Art (a later art pass)
- **City icon** that looks like a city (buildings or skyline in the owner's color), not a plain colored square.
- **Unit icons** that look like the unit: an infantry figure for Army, plane silhouettes, ship silhouettes, and so on, instead of letters.
